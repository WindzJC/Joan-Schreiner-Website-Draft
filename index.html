I.BEHIND THE TITLE

Not a story of having no scars.
A story of what comes after.

The title speaks to Joan's central message: pain is part of her story, but it is not the whole of it. Her Christian faith gives meaning to a journey that has included hardship, perseverance, and change.

This is not a promise of an easy life. It is a testimony about trusting God through difficult circumstances—and finding the courage to keep going.

THREE MOVEMENTS IN A TESTIMONY

THEMES, NOT CHAPTER TITLES
01
The wounds.
Acknowledging the seasons that changed her life, without allowing them to define her entire future.
02
The wrestling.
Holding onto faith while learning what endurance, forgiveness, and healing can ask of a person.
03
The renewal.
Seeing God's mercy and the possibility of restoration, even when the past cannot be undone.
